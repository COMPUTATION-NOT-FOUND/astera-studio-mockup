Astera Studio clickable mockup

Ask a question in plain words, get a chart with a short summary, pin it, and check the pipeline behind it. The same app runs in every frame below, and each layout adapts to its screen.

Open the app full screen Design note

You are on a narrow screen, so the device frames are hidden. The app itself is the phone layout.

Open the app

Frames are scaled to fit. Use “Open full size” for 100%.

Ask and pin

  1. Open Ask and tap “What explains the gap to target in Q3?”.
  2. Tap “Pin to dashboard” and pick a dashboard.
  3. Tap the menu (☰, “Chats” on desktop) to see earlier chats, or start a new one.

Focus the dashboard

  1. Tap “Global”, pick Europe on the globe, then “Show”.
  2. Tap the date range. Earlier years sit under Archive.
  3. Tap “+ New” to start another dashboard.

Fix a failed pipeline

  1. Two pipelines failed. Tap “Show” on the red line to see which.
  2. Open Regional targets. On iPhone, tap “Details” on step 3.
  3. Choose “Wait longer”, then “Save and re-run”.

The action dial

  1. Tap the filled circle for actions on this screen.
  2. Tap “⋯” on a chart or step for its own actions.
  3. Search, the last circle, reaches everything else.
